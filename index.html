Why it matters

Every smile tells a bigger story

For many children and adults, that story includes challenges with breathing, sleep, and overall health. That’s why we specialize in airway orthodontics in Massachusetts.

When the airway is narrow or blocked, it can affect breathing during the day and at night.

Orthodontic treatments like expanders or braces don’t just create space for teeth. They can also create more room for the airway, leading to easier breathing and better sleep.

For parents, this means orthodontics can impact more than appearance. It can support your child’s growth, energy, and focus. For adults, it can ease health concerns like snoring or sleep apnea.

What is airway orthodontics?

More than straightening teeth

Airway orthodontics focuses on how the mouth, jaw, and airway work together.

Children are often the first to show signs of airway-related concerns, but the symptoms can be easy to miss. Snoring or mouth breathing at night might not seem serious, but these can point to issues with jaw development or airway size.

Signs that can be easy to miss

  • Snoring at night
  • Mouth breathing
  • Restless sleep
  • Bedwetting
  • Trouble concentrating at school
  • Daytime fatigue

Airway orthodontics can guide jaw growth in a healthier way. With tools like palatal expanders, we can gently create more space for the airway, helping kids breathe through their nose and sleep more soundly. Parents often notice that after treatment, their child feels more rested, focused, and energetic during the day.

1

Palatal expanders

Used mainly in children, expanders gently widen the upper jaw to create more room for teeth and airflow.

2

Mandibular advancement devices

These appliances adjust jaw position to keep the airway open, especially helpful for adults with sleep apnea.

3

Functional appliances

These guide jaw growth in younger patients, supporting both facial balance and breathing.
