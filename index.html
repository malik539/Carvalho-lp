What is airway orthodontics?

Breathing, sleep, and long-term health

Airway orthodontics focuses on how the development of the jaw, teeth, and airway impacts breathing, sleep, and long-term health.

If your child snores, breathes through their mouth, or struggles with restless sleep, these may be signs of airway issues. Left untreated, these problems can affect growth, focus, energy, and overall well-being.

  • Snoring
  • Mouth breathing
  • Restless sleep
